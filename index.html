Sisu Offshore

Trust - Cooperation - Safety

The Sisu Offshore model brings together the high-performance capabilities of our partners into one integrated project management approach. By selecting the most suitable vessels, equipment and personnel for each project, we combine high performance with cost efficiency. We support our clients from the very beginning of a project, taking care of the entire process – from planning, permitting and seabed surveys to the construction of the connection, operational readiness, protection and maintenance for decades to come. Our approach provides transparent costs and strong project control throughout the entire lifecycle. By combining the expertise of our partners with defence-based risk management, we give our clients confidence that their critical maritime infrastructure is in capable hands.
